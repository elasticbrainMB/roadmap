# CONTEXT.md — why this roadmap exists, and how to write about it

**v1, 2026-09-05.** Read alongside `STATE.md`, `PRINCIPLES.md`, and
`CLAUDE.md` — those three are about running the projects; this one is about
why they're being documented at all. It sits at the root, not inside a
single project's own files, because it applies to more than one of them.

## 1. The newsletter this roadmap feeds

Matt writes **Elastic Brain** (`elasticbrain.beehiiv.com`), a newsletter
about his AI builds. [`Start by Starting`](https://elasticbrain.beehiiv.com/p/start-by-starting)
is the first post; [`About Me`](https://elasticbrain.beehiiv.com/p/about-me)
is his own framing of who he is and why he writes it.

**Why it exists, in his own words (2026-09-05):** the newsletter is meant
to be a **living resume** of his AI work. Whatever AI-related work he does
at his day job (Digital Marketing) isn't his primary or even secondary
responsibility there — his employer is conservative on AI adoption — and
he can't discuss it openly on LinkedIn or in interviews anyway, because of
NDA limits. It would also look rudimentary next to what he builds outside
work. **The newsletter is where the real evidence of his AI skill lives**,
because this roadmap and the projects on it are what he can actually point
to.

**The goal behind that:** transition from his current employer to a more
progressive, AI-involved or AI-focused marketing role. He's pursuing
AI-related certifications toward this, working up to Profound's
["Marketing Engineer"](https://university.tryprofound.com/courses/marketing-engineering-concepts/modules/manifesto/lessons/the-rise-of-the-marketing-engineer)
certification — a role that manifesto describes as marketers who've taught
themselves to build systems and AI-driven workflows, sitting inside a
marketing team rather than outside it. That's close to what this whole
roadmap already is.

## 2. What that means for how projects here get written up

**Build and ship first, then write about it.** Matt's own ordering, stated
2026-09-05 — not the reverse. When a project here reaches a real
milestone, the newsletter post is about what he was trying to accomplish,
what he actually learned, and how his tech stack evolved along the way —
not a plan for what he intends to do.

**He anticipates thought-leadership posts too** — the emergence of the
Marketing Engineer role is one likely topic — but that's not the current
focus. Building and shipping is.

**Practically, for anyone (human or otherwise) reading this roadmap to draft
a post from it:** `STATE.md` and each project's own status file are the
record of what actually happened and when. Pull from those, not from a plan
or an intention — the same discipline this repo already applies to itself.
